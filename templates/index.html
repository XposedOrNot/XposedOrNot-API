What you can build with it

Every documented endpoint is live in the interactive docs, ready to try straight from your browser.

GET /v1/check-email/{email}

Email breach check

Find out instantly whether an email address appears in any known breach. A plain GET with no authentication, ideal for signup checks and personal alerts.

See it in the docs →
GET /v1/breach-analytics

Breach catalog & analytics

Pull the full catalog of {{ breaches_display }} breaches with GET /v1/breaches, covering what leaked, when, and how bad, or get a detailed exposure profile with risk metrics for any email.

See it in the docs →
POST /v1/domain-breaches

Domain monitoring

Verify a domain you own and get breach exposure reports and alerts for every address on it. Built for security teams and IT admins.
